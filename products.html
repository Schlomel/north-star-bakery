Our Products

At North Star Bakery, we offer a variety of freshly baked breads, pastries, and cakes. Our products are prepared with care and are perfect for everyday meals, celebrations, and special occasions.

Breads

Our breads are baked fresh and include traditional favorites as well as seasonal selections.

  • Classic Sourdough: A traditional loaf with a crisp crust and soft, flavorful center. Prices range from $6 to $9.
  • Whole Wheat Bread: A hearty bread made for sandwiches, toast, or enjoying with your favorite meal. Prices range from $5 to $8.
  • Artisan Bread: Handmade specialty breads featuring different flavors and ingredients. Prices range from $7 to $12.

Pastries

Start your morning with one of our freshly baked pastries or enjoy one as an afternoon treat.

  • Croissants: Light and flaky pastries baked fresh each morning. Prices range from $3 to $5.
  • Muffins: Available in a variety of traditional and seasonal flavors. Prices range from $3 to $4.
  • Cinnamon Rolls: Soft rolls filled with cinnamon and topped with a sweet glaze. Prices range from $4 to $6.

Cakes

Our cakes can help make birthdays, anniversaries, and other celebrations even more special.

  • Celebration Cakes: Cakes available in several flavors and sizes. Prices range from $25 to $50.
  • Custom Cakes: Special-order cakes designed for birthdays, anniversaries, and other events. Prices start at $40.
  • Cupcakes: Individual treats available in classic and seasonal flavors. Prices range from $3 to $5 each.
