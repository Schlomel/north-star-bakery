About North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery was created with a simple goal: to provide fresh, handmade baked goods in a warm and welcoming neighborhood environment. We enjoy bringing people together through breads, pastries, cakes, and other freshly baked favorites.

Whether customers are stopping in on their way to work, picking up something for their family, or planning a special event, we want every visit to feel friendly and personal.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baked goods begin with quality ingredients. Whenever possible, North Star Bakery chooses fresh ingredients and works with suppliers that share our commitment to quality.

We also believe in reducing unnecessary waste and carefully preparing our products in quantities that help us provide customers with fresh baked goods throughout the day.
